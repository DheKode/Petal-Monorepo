PETAL / ORIGINAL SYSTEM

A little space.
A clear next step.

A flat, modern foundation for calm, useful interfaces. Rose gives actions a clear home; whitespace gives information room to breathe.

The six brand colors, Geist Mono, and blossom identity are approved. Sizes, component styling, and motion below are proposals for review. Examples use fictional content and save nothing.

04 / PROPOSED SHAPES & LAYERS

Soft edges. Flat surfaces.

Buttons use a 12px rounded rectangle and at least 48px height. Circles belong to compact symbols; sheets get more generous corners. No decorative shadows.

05 / PROPOSED MEASUREMENTS

Clear at small sizes.

The supplied blossom illustrates the 20, 24, and 32px size slots. It is brand artwork; the UI icon family still needs selection. Outline weight candidate: 1.75px.

06 / PROPOSED FEEDBACK

Motion with a reason.

Short transitions explain a state change. Nothing loops or plays automatically. Easing 0.2, 0, 0, 1; entry offset 8px; press scale 0.98.
