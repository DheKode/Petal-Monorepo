# Artwork provenance

The user approved the polished open blossom and custom wordmark in this design conversation. Flo was a study reference; its artwork and lettering were not used in this asset bundle.

`petal-logo-approved-reference.png` is the accepted presentation image, created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Its upper lockup supplies the production outlines. `petal-logo-transparent-extraction.png` was created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this handoff using the following prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction / precise-object-edit. Asset type: final Petal horizontal logo on genuine transparent alpha. Input is the APPROVED FINAL identity presentation. Extract ONLY THE UPPER HORIZONTAL LOCKUP: the rose three-petal open blossom on the left and the exact custom dark-plum lowercase petal wordmark on the right. Remove entire white background and both lower standalone examples. Do not redesign, reinterpret, retouch, reletter or change outlines. Keep the exact upper blossom contours, proportions, gaps, placement and approved wordmark's almond counters, p descender, e aperture, hooked t, single-storey a, curved l, tight spacing. Master centered, large, fully visible with modest clearspace, landscape canvas. Flatten all fills into solid EXACT rose #B83D70 blossom and deep plum #33252D wordmark. No texture, no gradients, no shadows, no lighting. All negative spaces/background fully transparent including letter counters and blossom gaps. Text verbatim petal. This is a production extraction of the accepted logo, no extra marks, variants, labels or annotations.

Production vectors were traced directly from the approved reference to preserve its proportions rather than adopt any generative extraction drift. Small raster edge irregularities were smoothed into quadratic vector curves. This is a vector reconstruction of raster artwork, not an original editable Figma source. Both source images are preserved for comparison.

Color, arrangement, PNG sizes, and the variation sheet are deterministic exports from the versioned vector outlines in `petal-logo-master.json`, using `export-assets.mjs`. No additional image-generation calls are required for these exports.
