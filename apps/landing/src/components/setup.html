A few steps.
Then, your routine.

These are unsigned prototype builds. Your device may ask you to confirm the installation.

Android
  1. Download the .apk on your phone.
  2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser or file app to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Petal.
Windows
  1. Download and run the .exe installer.
  2. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Petal from the Start menu.
macOS · Apple Silicon
  1. Open the .dmg and drag Petal into Applications.
  2. If blocked, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm once; later launches open normally.

Once you're in, confirm your product, choose a first or current pack, and set your usual time. Past days you leave blank stay marked as no record.
